Q05 | JOIN | 주문 목록과 주문한 회원의 이름을 함께 조회한다.
실행 시각: 2026-10-29T13:26:33+09:00
SQLite: 3.53.4
원본: sql/03_queries.sql
DB: data/shop.db

[실행 SQL]
SELECT o.id AS order_id, m.name AS member_name, o.ordered_at
FROM orders AS o
INNER JOIN member AS m ON m.id = o.member_id
ORDER BY o.id ASC;
[실제 결과]
order_id | member_name | ordered_at
---------+-------------+-----------
