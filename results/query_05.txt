Q05 | JOIN | 주문과 주문한 회원의 이름을 함께 조회한다.
실행 시각: 2026-10-15T13:58:11+09:00
SQLite: 3.53.1
원본: sql/03_queries.sql
DB: data/shop.db

[실행 SQL]
SELECT o.id AS order_id, m.name AS member_name, o.ordered_at
FROM orders AS o
INNER JOIN member AS m ON m.id = o.member_id
ORDER BY o.id;
