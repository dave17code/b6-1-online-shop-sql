Q06 | JOIN | Show products and item totals for order 1.
Recorded: 2026-10-29T14:33:22+09:00
SQLite: 3.53.1
Database: data/shop.db
Source: sql/03_queries.sql
Order: Q01 to Q15 from the original sample data

[SQL]
SELECT product_name, quantity, price, quantity * price AS item_total
FROM order_items
INNER JOIN products USING (product_id)
WHERE order_id = 1
ORDER BY order_item_id;
