Q11 | AGGREGATE | Find the average quantity per order-item row for each sold product.
Recorded: 2026-10-29T14:33:22+09:00
SQLite: 3.53.1
Database: data/shop.db
Source: sql/03_queries.sql
Order: Q01 to Q15 from the original sample data

[SQL]
SELECT product_name, AVG(quantity) AS average_quantity
FROM products
INNER JOIN order_items USING (product_id)
GROUP BY product_id, product_name
ORDER BY product_id;
